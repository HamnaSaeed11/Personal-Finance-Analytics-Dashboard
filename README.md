# Personal Finance Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview

The **Personal Finance Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive financial reporting solution designed to monitor personal spending behavior, analyze income, and understand expense distribution across multiple categories.

The dashboard transforms financial transaction data into meaningful visual insights, helping users track their expenses, identify major spending areas, and evaluate their overall financial activity.

It provides an organized view of living expenses, discretionary spending, transportation, dining, charity, and medical expenses through interactive charts and filters.


## Key Features

- **Expense Tracking:** Monitor total expenses across different months.
- **Income Analysis:** Visualize monthly net income trends.
- **Category-wise Analysis:** Compare spending across financial categories.
- **Account Analysis:** Understand the distribution of total spending by account.
- **Subcategory Analysis:** Identify the top five spending subcategories.
- **Monthly Trends:** Analyze changes in spending over time.
- **Financial Breakdown:** Compare increases, decreases, and total amounts by category.
- **Interactive Filtering:** Filter financial information by category and month.

## Key Dashboard Components

1. All Expenses – Monthly expense tracking.
2. Total Spend by Account – Account-level spending distribution.
3. Expenses by Category – Treemap visualization of expense allocation.
4. Net Income – Monthly income analysis.
5. Expenses by Category – Comparative spending trends.
6. Top 5 Subcategories – Identification of major spending areas.
7. Amount by Category – Financial increase and decrease analysis.
